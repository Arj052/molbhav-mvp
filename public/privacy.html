Privacy policy

MolBhav is an early-access service for restaurants and cloud kitchens in Pune. This page explains what we collect when you sign up, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Account details you give us: your name, email address, and, if you choose, your business name, business type and city.
  • If you use “Continue with Google”: your name, email address and Google account ID, shared by Google with your permission. We do not receive your Google password or access to your Gmail, Drive or contacts.
  • Sign-in activity: when you sign in and how many times, so we can see how early access is being used.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To contact you about MolBhav early access and your weekly price brief.

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

How we protect it

Passwords are stored only as secure one-way hashes. Sign-in uses secure, HTTP-only cookies. Only the MolBhav founder can view the list of people who have signed up.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time by emailing malikarjun725@gmail.com. If you signed in with Google, you can also remove MolBhav’s access from your Google Account settings.
